BRICKDRIFT

← → move   ↓ soft drop
Space hard drop
↑ / X rotate right   Z rotate left
C / Shift hold   P / Esc pause   L leaderboard
Gamepads work too: D-pad, A/B rotate, bumpers hold.
Tap the board to rotate
Drag left / right to move, drag down to soft drop
Flick down to hard drop, flick up to hold
Or use the buttons below the board.
